## Accepted boundaries and minimality

D01 licence acceptance covers mapped project-authored prose/software and public
synthetic conformance, with organism/artwork/naming exclusions retained. D02–D11,
D13 and D14 derive from synthetic delegation, not actual organism authorization.
D12 pins a public RFC8032 signer, one public adaptation fixture and local archives.
The real candidate needs an explicit reviewed applicability proposal and creator
choices; neither invent a new numbered decision nor silently expand old scope.

Minimal sufficient origin has no prior experience, synapse, children or fabricated
personality. Empty initial history does not waive demonstration of future causal
behavior, lineage or replay semantics. Blockchain, real sensors/hardware, private
memory, autonomous external breeding, indefinite ecology, sentience and market
value are not added prerequisites. D14 is required only for a selected Darwinian
selection claim, which can remain explicitly unproven. Key-loss fail-closed is an
acceptable proposed recovery policy if explicitly reviewed, not an invented need
for trustee infrastructure. Two local archives do not prove external retention;
independent witness is about the stated publication procedure, not an invented
blockchain or mandatory timestamp vendor.

A readiness checker may assess engineering completeness and candidate proposals.
It must fail closed on missing candidate/evidence and must not count self-reported
PASS fields, fixture signatures, a PR merge, test counts, or copied documentation
as evidence of actual attribution, retained archives, accepted rights or birth.
