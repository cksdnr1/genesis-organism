# Evolution

STATUS: RESEARCH. **NORMATIVE:** experience/environment MAY influence future
state only under explicit rules; genesis and valid history MUST remain intact.
Protocol evolution, implementation upgrades and organism evolution are separate.

A future transition rule needs defined inputs, validity checks, authorization,
resulting state and version. Heritable changes must be distinguished from memory
or transient expression. “Epigenetic” is currently a metaphor.

**OPEN QUESTIONS:** what changes, why changes are meaningful, who authorizes
them, how randomness is generated/recorded, and how effects are reproduced
across implementations. Random visual variation alone does not establish
adaptation or biological evolution. LLM-generated changes are proposals until
explicit deterministic acceptance rules establish their canonical meaning.

No mutation function, fitness objective, score or stochastic mechanism is
selected. Phase 4 follows a replayable model and defined experience semantics.
